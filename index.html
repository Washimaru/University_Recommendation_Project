How the matching works

A dual-layer model: hard constraints remove the impossible, then a similarity score ranks everything that's left by genuine fit.

1

Hard boundaries

Your major must be offered and the estimated net price must sit under your ceiling. Anything that fails is dropped — no false hope.

2

Vibe vectors

Every school carries a 6-dimension culture profile. Your sliders build a matching vector, and cosine similarity scores how aligned you are.

3

Soft nudges

Region, campus setting, class size, budget headroom and community fit adjust the ranking — they shape order, never exclude.

4

Explore

Sort, filter by reach/target/safety, save favorites, and compare up to three side-by-side with full vibe breakdowns.
